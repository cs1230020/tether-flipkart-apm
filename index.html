Survivor flow

Day 1, 07:40. Priya, 29, Ghaziabad. Her neighbour Ramesh vanished last night. She knows his mother is bedridden and diabetic.

  2. She checks in and marks who in her contacts is gone.

Responder flow

Anita is a school teacher who verified with ID and a selfie on Day 1. She sees one task at a time, ranked by the priority score.

  1. The case goes to the 3 nearest responders. The first to accept gets the address.
  2. The address unlocks only after she accepts.
  3. She logs "found" and the needs, then hands over to a named guardian with a photo log.
  4. If nobody marks the case "found" within 60 minutes, it's re-routed and escalated to command.
Child cases need two responders or a live photo handover. Every handover is visible to command.
